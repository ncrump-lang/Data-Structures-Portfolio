## About Me
My name is Nate Crump, and I am a junior at UNC Charlotte studying Sports Analytics. I am originally from Cameron, North Carolina, and grew up in Chapel Hill. Sports have always been a major part of my life, especially basketball, which I played throughout my childhood and all four years of high school. At the same time, I have always enjoyed working with numbers and was especially interested in math growing up. Studying Sports Analytics has given me the opportunity to combine those two interests while developing a deeper understanding of how data can be used within the sports industry.

Throughout my academic experience, I have developed skills in Python, R, Excel, statistics, and data visualization. My coursework has allowed me to apply these skills to real data and learn how to turn numbers into meaningful information. Outside of the classroom, my experiences as a delivery driver, leadership intern at a camp, volunteer, and student-athlete have helped me become a dependable person and a strong teammate. I believe those experiences have also strengthened my communication, leadership, and ability to work well with others.

After graduating from UNC Charlotte, my goal is to begin a career as a sports data analyst or pursue another opportunity within the data science field, preferably one connected to sports. I am also interested in potentially attending graduate school to continue my education. Ultimately, I hope to build a career that allows me to combine my analytical abilities with my passion for sports.

Outside of academics and career goals, I enjoy playing basketball, exercising, playing guitar and piano, creating and listening to music, and playing video games. In the next five years, I hope to be established in a career that I enjoy, continuing to grow professionally while doing work that genuinely interests me.
This website will document my learning and projects throughout the semester.
